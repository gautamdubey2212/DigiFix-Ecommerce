DigiFix InfoTech primarily provides digital software products, cloud-based solutions, cybersecurity services, and IT consulting services. Since these are digital services, refunds are subject to specific conditions.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered only under the following situations:

Refunds will not be applicable in the following situations:

Customers requesting a refund must contact DigiFix InfoTech with transaction details, payment proof, and reason for the request.

Approved refunds, if applicable, may take approximately 5 to 10 business days to reflect in the original payment method, depending on the payment gateway or banking provider.
