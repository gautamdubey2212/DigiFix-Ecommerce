Digital Delivery Policy

DigiFix InfoTech delivers digital software products, cloud solutions, cybersecurity services, and IT-based systems electronically through secure digital channels.

This Digital Delivery Policy explains how DigiFix InfoTech processes, activates, and delivers software products, cloud services, cybersecurity systems, and digital IT solutions purchased through our platform.

DigiFix InfoTech does not provide physical shipping or courier-based delivery. All products and services are delivered digitally through secure online methods including email, dashboard access, cloud deployment, software activation, and digital credentials.

1. Delivery Methods

Depending on the purchased service or product, digital delivery may be completed through:

  • Email delivery of software credentials or activation keys
  • Secure dashboard or client portal access
  • Cloud deployment and configuration
  • API access credentials and system integration
  • Remote software installation or setup support
  • Digital account creation and onboarding

2. Delivery Timeframe

Delivery timelines may vary depending on the type of software product, cloud service, customization level, or technical implementation requirements.

  • Standard digital products: within 24 hours
  • Cloud or security service activation: 1 to 3 business days
  • Custom IT solutions or deployment services: based on project scope and agreement
  • Enterprise integrations: timeline communicated separately during onboarding

3. Delivery Confirmation

Customers may receive delivery confirmation through:

  • Official email communication
  • Client dashboard notifications
  • Activation credentials or access links
  • Deployment completion reports

4. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing accurate email addresses, contact information, technical requirements, and business details necessary for successful digital delivery.

  • Maintain valid communication details
  • Ensure compatibility of systems or devices
  • Provide required project information on time
  • Protect login credentials and account access

5. Failed Delivery Situations

DigiFix InfoTech shall not be held responsible for delivery delays or failures caused by:

  • Incorrect email addresses or contact details
  • Spam filters or blocked communications
  • Customer-side technical limitations
  • Third-party hosting or network issues
  • Incomplete onboarding information from the customer

6. Support & Assistance

DigiFix InfoTech provides technical support related to software activation, account access, onboarding, and deployment assistance during applicable support periods.

7. Service Availability

Certain cloud services, cybersecurity systems, and enterprise-level solutions may require scheduled maintenance, upgrades, or temporary downtime for operational improvements.

8. International Digital Delivery

DigiFix InfoTech may provide digital services and software solutions to customers globally, subject to applicable laws, compliance requirements, and technical feasibility.

9. Policy Updates

DigiFix InfoTech reserves the right to modify or update this Digital Delivery Policy at any time without prior notice.
